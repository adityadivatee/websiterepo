Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective for Aditya Divte Production (adityadivte.com) and affiliated platforms.

At Aditya Divte Production, we are committed to upholding transparency, privacy rights, and responsible data management practices across all our applications, services, and online resources.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy details how Aditya Divte Production ("we", "our", or "us"), founded by Aditya Divte and operating under adityadivte.com, collects, processes, and protects personal and non-personal data when you navigate our website, utilize our software solutions (such as Divte Vault, Webecon, Divte Form, and DivteERP), or access our subdomains.

By accessing or continuing to browse adityadivte.com, you consent to the practices outlined in this policy. If you disagree with any terms, please discontinue using our platforms.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information through various touchpoints:

  • Directly Provided Information: When you submit inquiries through our contact forms, request software demonstrations, or correspond with support, we may record your name, email address, telephone number, organization details, and project requirements.
  • Automated Device & Technical Data: When browsing our pages, our servers automatically log technical metadata such as your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser family, operating system version, referring URLs, screen resolution, and time spent on specific sections.
  • Application Usage Analytics: Anonymized metrics on which products, tool showcases, or articles are viewed to enhance site performance and reader engagement.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

To support the development and maintenance of our open web tools and software documentation, we partner with third-party advertising platforms, including Google AdSense. Please review the following mandatory disclosures regarding third-party advertising cookies:

  • Third-Party Vendors & Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.
  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to adityadivte.com and other websites.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry Opt-Out Platforms: Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative / AboutAds Opt-Out Page.

4. Cookies & Web Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text packets saved to your browser cache. In addition to advertising cookies, we may utilize:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for fundamental site navigation, security token verification, and form transmission.
  • Analytical / Performance Cookies: Used to understand traffic volume, popular reading sections, and page load latencies so we can optimize rendering performance.

You retain the right to disable or filter cookies through your individual browser preferences (e.g., in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge). Note that disabling certain essential cookies may affect interactive features.

5. How We Use Collected Information

Any information gathered is strictly used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and enhance our software products, game releases, and educational materials.
  • Respond to client requests, enterprise quotes, and technical support inquiries.
  • Maintain network security, prevent fraudulent bot traffic, and defend against denial-of-service vulnerabilities.
  • Comply with applicable legal mandates and statutory regulations.

6. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards—including TLS/SSL cryptographic encryption in transit—to protect your communication and submitted data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

7. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Regardless of your geographic location, we respect your rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: You can request confirmation regarding whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of any personal records you provided via our contact channels.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

8. External Links & Subdomains

Our website may contain hyperlinks to external portfolios, social channels, or subdomains (e.g., pixasboard.adityadivte.com). Each dedicated subdomain or external platform maintains its own specific terms and privacy disclosures. We encourage you to review their policies upon visiting.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this statement to reflect changes in our technology stack, advertising networks, or regulatory mandates. The "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document will always indicate the latest revision date.
